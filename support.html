Frequently Asked Questions

How to file a Jan Shikayat (Civic Complaint)? ▼
Go to the Civic module from the bottom categories or home page, fill in the issue category, upload an optional photo proof, and submit. You will get a tracking ID instantly.
How do I use CP Wallet balance? ▼
Your CP Wallet balance can be used to pay for local marketplace orders, municipal services, or transferred directly to your bank account via UPI.
What to do in case of an Emergency? ▼
Use the Emergency SOS tab in your profile. Holding the SOS button for 3 seconds automatically triggers GPS live location sharing with your saved family contacts and local authorities.
How to change app language? ▼
Go to Profile ➔ App Settings & Privacy ➔ App Language, and select your preferred regional language (English, Hindi, Bengali).
