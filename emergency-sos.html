Citizen Legal Rights & Safety Guide
Right to File Zero FIR
A police station cannot refuse to register an FIR based on jurisdiction. You can file a Zero FIR anywhere.
Women's Arrest Rights
Women cannot be arrested before 6 AM and after 6 PM except in special cases with magistrate's permission.
Traffic Police Checks
Only an officer of Sub-Inspector (SI) rank or above can penalize you for traffic violations.
